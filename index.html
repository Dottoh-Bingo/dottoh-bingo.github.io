New Random Board draws 24 tiles from the enabled pool, then arranges them so every row and column takes a similar number of hours. Untick tiles you don't want drawn. Tiles marked est. aren't in TempleOSRS's boss rates, so their hours are estimates. Share a board with your clan using the board code below the board.

Rows, columns and both diagonals count as a bingo. The centre square is a free space and always counts as complete. "Any N" tiles accept duplicates. Hours are expected time at TempleOSRS's Ironman EHB kill rates × drop rates; most players will take longer, but every row and column is balanced to a similar total. Solo progress is saved in this browser. Create or join a team to share one board and its ticks live with your teammates. Click a completed tile again to unmark it.
